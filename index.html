Global Tourism & Air Transport Safety Forecasting
Complete
Final year major project · 4-member team · Jul 2025 – Apr 2026

Does tourism demand track air transport intensity — and can it be forecast reliably? I integrated six real-world datasets covering tourist arrivals and departures, air passenger volumes and aviation safety indicators, then built and compared three forecasting approaches rather than defending a single one.

  • Built a reproducible pandas preprocessing pipeline — country-name standardisation across inconsistent sources, forward/backward-fill imputation, duplicate aggregation, IQR-based outlier capping — with the rationale documented for every treatment rather than records dropped silently.
  • Engineered year-over-year growth rates, indexed trend series, volatility measures and lag sequences for supervised time-series learning.
  • Trained ARIMA, Prophet and an LSTM network, then recommended the most interpretable model for the use case — not simply the lowest error.
  • Presented model comparisons to an academic review panel, translating statistical output into plain language for non-specialists.
